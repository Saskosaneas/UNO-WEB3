# UNO — Official Rules

> **Players:** 2 to 10  
> **Ages:** 7+  
> **Contents:** 112 Cards  

---

## Contents of the Deck

A standard UNO deck consists of **112 cards**:

* **76 Numbered Cards (0–9):**
  * 19 Blue cards (0–9)
  * 19 Green cards (0–9)
  * 19 Red cards (0–9)
  * 19 Yellow cards (0–9)
* **24 Action Cards:**
  * 8 **Skip** cards (2 each in Blue, Green, Red, Yellow)
  * 8 **Reverse** cards (2 each in Blue, Green, Red, Yellow)
  * 8 **Draw 2** cards (2 each in Blue, Green, Red, Yellow)
* **8 Wild Cards:**
  * 4 **Wild** cards
  * 4 **Wild Draw 4** cards
* **4 Blank Cards** (Custom / Replacement)

---

## Object of the Game

Be the first player to get rid of all cards in your hand in each round. You score points for the cards your opponents are left holding. The first player to score **500 points** wins the game.

---

## Setup

1. **Choose a Dealer:** Each player draws a card from the deck. The player with the highest point value deals (Action/Wild cards count as zero for this draw).
2. **Deal:** Shuffle the deck. Each player is dealt **7 cards**.
3. **Draw Pile:** Place the remaining cards face down in the center to form the **DRAW pile**.
4. **Discard Pile:** Flip over the top card of the DRAW pile to begin the **DISCARD pile**.
   * *Note:* If the first card flipped is a **Wild** or **Wild Draw 4**, return it to the deck, shuffle, and draw another card. For other special cards turned up, see [Special Cards](#special-cards).

---

## Gameplay

1. The player to the left of the dealer goes first. Play initially proceeds clockwise (to the left).
2. On your turn, you must match the top card on the DISCARD pile by **color**, **number**, or **symbol/action word**.
   * *Example:* If the card is a Green 7, you may play any green card or a 7 of any color.
   * You may also play an eligible **Wild** or **Wild Draw 4** card on any turn.
3. **If you have no matching card:** You must take one card from the DRAW pile.
   * If the drawn card can be legally played, you may play it immediately.
   * Otherwise, your turn ends and play moves to the next player.
4. **Reneging:** You may choose *not* to play a playable card from your hand. If so, you must draw a card from the DRAW pile. If that newly drawn card is playable, it may be played immediately, but you cannot play any card from your hand on that turn.

## Special Cards

| Card | Action / Rules |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Draw 2** | Next player must draw 2 cards and forfeit their turn. If turned up at the start of the game, the first player draws 2 cards and is skipped. Must match color or another Draw 2. |
| **Reverse** | Reverses the direction of play (clockwise becomes counter-clockwise, and vice versa). If turned up at start, the dealer plays first and play continues to the right. Must match color or another Reverse. |
| **Skip** | The next player in turn is skipped and forfeits their turn. If turned up at start, the player to the dealer's left is skipped and the next player starts. Must match color or another Skip. |
| **Wild** | Changes the active color to any color of your choice (including the current color). Can be played even if you have another valid card in hand. If turned up at start, the player to the left of the dealer chooses the starting color. |
| **Wild Draw 4** | Calls the next color and forces the next player to draw 4 cards and skip their turn. **Hitch:** Can only be played if you do **not** have a card in hand matching the *color* of the discard pile (matching numbers/action cards of another color do not prevent play). Cannot be the starting card of the game. |
| **Blank Card** | Can be used as a replacement for lost/damaged cards or marked with a custom house rule agreed upon before playing. |

### Challenging a Wild Draw 4
If a player suspects a **Wild Draw 4** was played illegally (i.e., the player holds a card matching the discard pile's color), only the affected player (the one forced to draw 4) may issue a **challenge**:
* The challenged player must privately show their hand to the challenger.
* **If Guilty:** The challenged player must draw the 4 cards instead.
* **If Innocent:** The challenger must draw the 4 cards **plus 2 additional cards** (6 total) and forfeits their turn.

## Calling "UNO!"

* When playing your **second-to-last card**, you must shout **"UNO!"** before the card touches the discard pile.
* **Penalty:** If you fail to say "UNO" and another player catches you before the next player begins their turn, you must draw **4 penalty cards**.
* **Timing:**
  * You cannot be penalized until your second-to-last card touches the discard pile.
  * If you catch yourself before any other player speaks up, you are safe.
  * A player has until the next player "begins their turn" (either drawing from the deck or playing a card from hand) to call you out. Once the next turn begins, no penalty can be applied.

---

## Going Out & Scoring

A round ends when a player successfully plays their final card.

* If the last card played is a **Draw 2** or **Wild Draw 4**, the next player must still draw those cards before scores are tallied.
* If the DRAW pile runs out before anyone goes out, reshuffle the DISCARD pile (leaving the top card) to form a new DRAW pile.

### Winning
* **Standard Scoring:** The first player to reach **500 points** across rounds wins the game.
* **Alternative Scoring (Elimination / Lowest Score):** Keep a running total of the points remaining in each player's hand at the end of each round. When a player reaches 500 points, they are eliminated (or the player with the lowest score at that point wins).

## Game Variations

### Two Players
When playing with only 2 players:
1. **Reverse** acts like a **Skip**: playing a Reverse allows you to immediately take another turn.
2. Playing a **Skip** allows you to immediately play another card.
3. When you play a **Draw 2** or **Wild Draw 4**, your opponent draws the cards and play immediately returns to you.

### Partner Play (4 Players)
* Partners sit across from each other.
* When either partner plays their last card, the hand is over.
* Add up the points remaining in both opponents' hands and award that combined score to the winning team.

### Multi-Table Tournaments
* **4 Players:** Play 4 hands with each of the other 3 players as your partner (12 hands total). Keep track of individual points accumulated across partnerships.
* **8 Players:** Run 2 separate tables where each player partners with every other player for 4 hands each (28 hands total).

### Challenge UNO (Elimination)
* Track running totals of points caught in each player's hand.
* Once a player reaches a designated threshold (typically 500 points), they are eliminated.
* When only two players remain, they play head-to-head under two-player rules. The last surviving player wins.
